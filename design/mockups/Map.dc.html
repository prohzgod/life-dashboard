3 · Local-first data

This device (source of truth)works with no internet
App UI
Instant reads & writes
Encrypted local database
Tasks, goals, habits, notes
File store
Images, attachments, thumbnails
Sync engine
Change log + merge (CRDT). Offline edits queue and merge on reconnect, conflicts are kept, never lost.
Your other devices (optional)
Via an end-to-end encrypted relay, your own server, or nothing at all. The relay only ever sees ciphertext.
Backups · daily encrypted snapshot to a folder, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or S3. Export: Markdown + images, JSON, CSV.
Integrations · calendars (read-only), Apple Health / Health Connect through the phone app, bank CSV import.
AI · on-device model by default, or your own API key. You choose which areas it may read.
